# Code of conduct

We make videos with agents, together. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their background, identity or experience.

- Be kind and specific. Critique the card, the command, the clip; never the person.
- Credit people. Name the maker of every example and every idea you build on.
- Respect rights. No pirated media, no faces or voices without consent, no living artist's name as a style.
- Keep ads honest. No fake "I used it" claims, no health or income promises, AI labels where platforms require them.
- No harassment, no slurs, no spam, no self-promotion that is not a real skill.

Maintainers may edit, close or remove anything that breaks these rules and may block repeat offenders. Report a problem to conduct@varg.ai; we read everything and keep it confidential.

# Stacked Cards Fan Out

A pile of cards or photos shuffles, then fans or settles one forward into focus.

- **Type:** Effects · Galleries
- **Status:** live
- **Publisher:** Codrops
- **License:** MIT (per demo repo)

- **Full card, kept current:** <https://varg.ai/how-to/skills/stacked-cards-fan.md>

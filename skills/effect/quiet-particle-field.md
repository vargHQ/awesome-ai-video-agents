# Quiet Particle Field

Out-of-focus dots drift slowly in depth behind the content, adding life without motion noise.

- **Type:** Effects · Backdrops
- **Status:** live
- **Publisher:** tsParticles (Matteo Bruni)
- **License:** MIT

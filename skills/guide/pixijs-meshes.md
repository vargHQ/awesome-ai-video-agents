# PixiJS Meshes

Deform images with custom geometry in PixiJS v8: meshes, planes, ropes and 2.5D perspective corners.

- **Type:** Guides · Shaders
- **Status:** live
- **Publisher:** PixiJS
- **License:** MIT
- **Source:** <https://github.com/pixijs/pixijs-skills/tree/main/skills/pixijs-scene-mesh>
- **Works in:** Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI
- **Tags:** `mesh` `deformation` `2.5d`
- **Full card, kept current:** <https://varg.ai/how-to/skills/pixijs-meshes.md>
